Our Story

Founded in 2022, GoSmoothie started with a simple vision: to make healthy living accessible to everyone. What began as a small smoothie stand in a local market has grown into a thriving delivery service trusted by thousands.

Our founder, a fitness enthusiast and nutritionist, noticed a gap in the market for fresh, affordable smoothies delivered on-demand. With a passion for health and wellness, GoSmoothie was born.

Today, we're proud to serve our community with the finest blends, supporting local farmers, and inspiring thousands to embrace a healthier lifestyle.
