# Payment brand marks — provenance

Every file in this directory is vendored so the checkout renders from this project's own origin and
makes no third-party requests. Files were downloaded once, sanitised (`<script>`, `<foreignObject>`
and `on*` attributes removed) and capped at 40 KB.

**Nominative use.** Each mark is used only to identify the payment method it names, so a shopper can
recognise their app, card network or bank in the payment modal. No affiliation, sponsorship or
endorsement by any of these brands is claimed or implied. All names and logos remain the trademarks
of their respective owners. Any owner who would rather not appear here can have their mark removed by
deleting the file; the modal falls back to a lettered chip automatically.
